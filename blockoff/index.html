Blockoff

Arcade Puzzle

Line up the blocks. Ignite them. Launch them into space.

Coming soon to Google Play

Blockoff is an arcade puzzle game. Line up blocks of the same kind to ignite them, then launch whole stacks into space to collect materials and unlock new planets. No account, no purchases, and only optional ads you choose to watch.
