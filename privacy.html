Privacy Policy

For all games by yabogames · Last updated 24 November 2026

In short. Our games have no accounts and no servers of their own. Your scores, settings and progress stay on your phone. Some games show ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data. In the EU, UK and Switzerland, you choose whether that data may be used for personalised ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play. Section 9 lists what each game uses.

1. Who is responsible

Ada Yabanelli (yabogames)
Bahnhofstr. 20, 65795 Hattersheim, Germany
Email: gamesyabo@gmail.com

I make the games published under the name yabogames and am the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). I have not appointed a data protection officer and am not required to.

2. Data stored only on your phone

Each game saves things like your scores, statistics, settings, current game, unlocked content, which purchases you own, and when an ad was last shown, in the app's own storage on your phone. This data never leaves your phone. I cannot see it, and nothing is sent to me. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings. The games use no analytics and no tracking of their own.

3. Advertising with Google AdMob

Games that show ads use Google AdMob, provided in the EEA and UK by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and elsewhere by Google LLC, USA. When ads are loaded, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be estimated
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance and crashes

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and, only if you agree, to personalise ads. The data is encrypted in transit.

Your choice. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, a game with ads shows Google's consent message the first time you open it. If you agree, Google may use your data and store or read information on your device for personalised ads (legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). If you do not agree, you may still see ads that are not personalised; Google then processes data only as needed to serve those ads and prevent fraud (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest in funding the games). You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy policy → Privacy choices.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings. Google may process data in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases through Google Play

Optional purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. I never receive your payment details. A game only checks on your phone which items your Google account owns, so it can unlock them (legal basis: fulfilling the purchase, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

5. Reminders and sharing

If you allow notifications, a game can schedule reminders on your phone itself. No server is involved, and no data is sent. You can turn them off in the game or in Android settings. Share buttons open Android's own share menu with a short text; you decide whether and where to send it, and I receive nothing.

6. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly process their data.

7. How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it (see section 2). Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention rules, described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (in the game's Privacy choices). Because I hold no data about you on any server, requests about advertising data are best sent to Google, but you can always write to me and I will help. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one in your country or federal state.
