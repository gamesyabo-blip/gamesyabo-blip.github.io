Privacy Policy

For the Android game “Monoline – Shiroku” · Last updated 12 November 2026

In short. The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your scores, settings and unlocks stay on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data. In the EU, UK and Switzerland, you choose whether that data may be used for personalised ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

I am the developer of Monoline – Shiroku and the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). I have not appointed a data protection officer and am not required to.

2. Data stored only on your phone

The game saves the following in the app's own storage on your phone:

  • scores, leaderboard, game statistics and your current game
  • settings (sound, music, vibration, theme, daily reminder)
  • Daily puzzle results, unlocked themes, items and which purchases you own
  • when the last ad was shown, so ads are not shown too often

This data never leaves your phone. I cannot see it, and nothing is sent to me. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings. The game uses no analytics and no tracking of its own.

3. Advertising with Google AdMob

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, provided in the EEA and UK by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and elsewhere by Google LLC, USA. When ads are loaded, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be estimated
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance and crashes

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and, only if you agree, to personalise ads. The data is encrypted in transit.

Your choice. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent message the first time you open it. If you agree, Google may use your data and store or read information on your device for personalised ads (legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). If you do not agree, you may still see ads that are not personalised; Google then processes data only as needed to serve those ads and prevent fraud (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest in funding the game). You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings. Google may process data in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases through Google Play

Optional purchases (such as Ad-free) are processed entirely by Google Play. I never receive your payment details. The game only checks on your phone which items your Google account owns, so it can unlock them (legal basis: fulfilling the purchase, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

5. Daily reminder

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a daily reminder on your phone itself. No server is involved, and no data is sent. You can turn it off in the game's settings or in Android settings.

6. Sharing your result

The share button opens Android's own share menu with a short text about your Daily result. You decide whether and where to send it. I receive nothing.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly process their data.

8. How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it (see section 2). Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention rules, described in the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Settings → Privacy choices). Because I hold no data about you on any server, requests about advertising data are best sent to Google, but you can always write to me and I will help. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one in your country or federal state.

10. Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, I will update this page and the date at the top.
