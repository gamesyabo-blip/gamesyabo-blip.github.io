Zigline

Brain Connect Puzzle

Connect the ropes. Beat the rise.

Coming soon to Google Play

Rope pieces fill the board and new rows keep rising. Swap tiles to link a line from the left wall to the right wall; when it connects, the rope lights up and clears. Chain clears for Fever, pick a buff from every chest, and upgrade between runs. Plays offline, no account, and only optional ads you choose to watch.
